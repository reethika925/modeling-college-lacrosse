About Cradl

Live scores, ratings, and model picks for Division I men's lacrosse

What is Cradl?

Cradl is a data platform for NCAA Division I men's lacrosse. It puts live scores, odds, power ratings, team and player stats, and a statistical model's picks for every game in one place, on the web and on iPhone.

Cradl is not a sportsbook. We don't accept wagers or pay out winnings. Everything here is for informational and entertainment purposes only.

What's Here

  • Games: live scores, current odds, line movement, lines compared across sportsbooks, the model's picks, what other Cradl users picked, and a plain-language write-up for every game
  • Results: final scores and box scores, filtered by season and team
  • Rankings: power ratings for every D1 team, updated after each game. From November until opening day they show the preseason order
  • Bracket: a projected NCAA tournament field built from the selection criteria
  • Teams and players: records, splits, schedules, rosters, box-score stats, and player impact ratings
  • Pick'em: make your own picks and climb the leaderboard
  • Census: who plays D1 lacrosse, by size, class, hometown, and pipeline

The Model

Every team carries a power rating that moves after each game based on the result, the opponent, and where it was played. Between seasons, ratings are pulled part of the way back toward each team's conference average.

  • Spreads: the rating gap between two teams becomes an expected margin, adjusted by Process Quality, a box-score efficiency score
  • Totals: each team's scoring at home and on the road, adjusted for pace
  • Moneylines: the team the model rates as the likely winner

The model compares its numbers with the market line and takes a side. Its rules are fixed before the season starts and don't change mid-season.

How Picks Work

  • Every game, three markets: the model records a spread, total, and moneyline pick on every game it rates, locked soon after its line first posts. No hand-picking.
  • Locked at the first line: each pick locks within about an hour of that market's line first posting and never changes after, even if the line moves. A moneyline that never gets a price locks 75 minutes before faceoff.
  • Every result counts: each pick is graded against the final score and kept on the Picks tracker, wins and losses alike. Early-season totals are marked and kept out of the headline record.
  • AI write-ups: Claude writes a plain-language breakdown of why the model likes its side, using the same stats you see on the page. It explains the picks; it never chooses them.

Where the Data Comes From

  • Odds: licensed sportsbooks via The Odds API. Picks lock to the DraftKings line
  • Scores and stats: public NCAA game records, box scores, and team rosters
  • Write-ups: Anthropic's Claude
